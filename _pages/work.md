## Digital identity

pipe23 works on digital identity and digital sovereignty. The current work is tooling around the [Longfellow zero-knowledge proof system](https://eprint.iacr.org/2024/2010). pipe23 follows standards development and the rollouts of EU age verification and the EU Digital Identity Wallet.

The repositories are at [github.com/pipe23-org](https://github.com/pipe23-org).
