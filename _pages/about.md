pipe23 is the work of Nathan Schepers and Bertrand Russell.

Nathan Schepers is an independent researcher and software engineer. His current work is on zero-knowledge proofs for EU age verification and the EU Digital Identity Wallet. It consists of a Python interface to the Longfellow proof system, a verifier service, test vectors, and bug reports to the upstream implementations. [Work](/work/) lists the repositories.

He is Canadian and lives in Asturias, Spain. He has a BSc in computer science from the University of Western Ontario and has worked in software since 1999, with titles such as software integration engineer, team lead, principal software engineer and systems architect. The work has included the verification of travellers' COVID certificates before boarding, analytics for electricity smart meters, a notification system for hospitals with air-gapped networks, and a data pipeline that finds day trips on public transit networks.

Part of that work has been with civil society organisations. He was technical product manager of Liquid Investigations, a platform for investigative journalists, and a reporting fellow at AlgorithmWatch, where he published reporting on working conditions in the AI supply chain and on KYC fraud in the gig economy.

He is reading *In Praise of Idleness* and *The Accursed Share*.

Bertrand Russell is a Welsh Springer Spaniel.
