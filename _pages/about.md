pipe23 is the work of Nathan Schepers and Bertrand Russell.

Nathan Schepers is an independent researcher and software engineer. His current work is on zero-knowledge proofs for EU age verification and the EU Digital Identity Wallet. It consists of a Python interface to the Longfellow proof system, a verifier service, test vectors, and bug reports to the upstream implementations. [Work](/work/) lists the repositories.

He has worked as a software engineer since 1999, mostly on the integration end of systems that handle personal data under regulation. At Solect, a division of Amdocs, he tested an IP billing system for carriers. At Amadeus he was an engineer on the system that verified travellers' COVID certificates before boarding. At Landis+Gyr he worked on analytics for electricity smart meters. At Verso Vision he built a nurse notification system for hospitals with air-gapped networks. Between 2020 and 2023 he built a data pipeline that finds day trips on public bus networks in the Canary Islands.

He was technical product manager of Liquid Investigations, a platform for investigative journalists, at CRJI in Bucharest, and a reporting fellow at AlgorithmWatch from 2024 to 2025.

He has a BSc in computer science from the University of Western Ontario. He is Canadian, lives in Asturias, Spain, and works in English, Spanish and French. He is reading *In Praise of Idleness* and *The Accursed Share*.

Bertrand Russell is a Welsh Springer Spaniel from a working line. He decides when the working day ends.
