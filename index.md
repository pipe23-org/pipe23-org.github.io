pipe23 is the work of Nathan Schepers and Bertrand Russell. It focuses on public infrastructure research and engineering, currently on European digital identity.

**Nathan Schepers** is an independent researcher and software engineer. His current work is on zero-knowledge proofs for EU age verification and the EU Digital Identity Wallet. GitHub links can be found under [Work](/work/).

He has worked in software since 1999. His projects have included verification of travellers' COVID certificates, analytics for electricity smart meters, a hospital notification system, and public transit network analysis.

Part of his work has been with journalism and civil society organizations. He was technical product manager at [CRJI](https://crji.ro/), and a reporting fellow at [AlgorithmWatch](https://algorithmwatch.org/en/fellowship/), where he published reporting on working conditions in the AI supply chain and on KYC fraud in the gig economy.

He is currently reading _The Accursed Share_.

**Bertrand Russell** (b. 2025) is a Welsh Springer Spaniel. His current work is on whistle recall and steadiness.
